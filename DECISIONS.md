| 2026-11-04 | F2a | Moyasar webhook verification | Docs (`/api/other/webhooks/webhook-reference`, `/guides/dashboard/setting-up-webhooks`, `available-webhooks`, `create-webhook`): the body is `{id, type, created_at, secret_token, account_name, live, data}` where `data` is the payment; the **secret token is a shared secret in the JSON body**, no signature header or HMAC is documented. We compare `secret_token` with `Store:Payments:Moyasar:WebhookSecret` in **constant time** (`CryptographicOperations.FixedTimeEquals` over SHA-256 digests), reject a `live` flag that does not match the key mode, and **always re-fetch the payment** (`GET /payments/{id}`) before settling — the body is never trusted for amounts or status. Events handled: `payment_paid`, `payment_captured`, `payment_failed` (also the doc's typo `payment_faild`), `payment_voided`, `payment_refunded`; any other type → 200 + recorded `ignored`. Moyasar retries non-2xx 5 more times (1 min … 2 h); reconciliation covers anything lost | A shared-secret scheme is weaker than an HMAC; the re-fetch makes a forged body harmless beyond a lookup | REVIEW |
