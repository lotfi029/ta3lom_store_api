# 01 — Store Business Requirements

**Project:** ta3lom Store (متجر جمعية التعلم الإلكتروني)
**Revision:** S1 draft · 29 September 2026
**Prototype:** [`design/ta3lom-store-prototype.html`](../../design/ta3lom-store-prototype.html) — approved as the visual and behavioural reference
**Stack (decided):** ASP.NET Core (.NET 10) API · PostgreSQL 18 · Angular 22 feature inside `ta3lom-web` · Hostinger VPS

---

## 1. Why the store exists

The association already produces learning material — courses, books, printed kits — and already
depends on donations. Today these are two unrelated things, and neither works online:

1. **Nothing can be bought.** Courses and books are distributed by hand or not at all. There is no
   way for a teacher, parent or student to pay for one.
2. **Donations are abstract.** The main-site donate page (FR-40 to FR-45) accepts an amount, but a
   donor cannot say *what* the money buys. Donors respond much better to concrete, countable gifts
   — a water cooler, a tablet, *ten seats in a course* — with a goal and a progress bar. The
   reference store (store.kafeef.sa) is built entirely around this.
3. **Beneficiaries pay for learning they cannot afford.** The same course a professional buys for
   350 SAR is the one a rural teacher or an orphan-home student needs. The store should let one
   person pay so that another learns, and prove that the seat reached someone.

The store therefore joins **commerce** (courses, books) and **giving** (sponsorships, donation
products) in one catalogue, one cart and one checkout.

## 2. Business objectives

| ID | Objective | Measure (12 months after launch) |
|---|---|---|
| OBJ-1 | Generate earned income from the association's own content | ≥ 250,000 SAR net course and book sales |
| OBJ-2 | Turn donations into specific, trackable gifts | ≥ 60% of donation value arrives through a product (not the general fund) |
| OBJ-3 | Put paid learning in the hands of people who cannot pay | ≥ 2,000 sponsored seats allocated; ≥ 60% of allocated course seats completed |
| OBJ-4 | Earn donor trust through transparency | 100% of sponsored units show an allocation status to their sponsor within 30 days of funding |
| OBJ-5 | Reduce manual finance work | Receipts, zakat ledger and sales reports generated by the system; zero spreadsheet reconciliation |

## 3. Business requirements

Priorities use MoSCoW, as in [`Docs/requirements/README.md`](../requirements/README.md).

### BR-S01 — Sell courses online *(Must)*
A visitor can buy a course for themselves and gets access immediately after payment is confirmed.

### BR-S02 — Sell books in print and digital formats *(Must)*
Printed books ship within the Kingdom. Digital books download immediately and carry the buyer's watermark.

### BR-S03 — Sponsor seats and copies for others *(Must)*
For any course or book the association marks as sponsorable, a donor can pay for **N seats or
copies**, or contribute **any amount** that is pooled with other donors until a full seat is funded.
The association chooses the beneficiaries against published criteria. The donor never picks a person.

### BR-S04 — Donation products with goals *(Must)*
The association publishes concrete donation products (water coolers, tablets, data bundles, school
bags, a general education fund). Each one has an optional unit price, a target amount, an end date
and a public progress bar. A donor gives whole units or any amount.

### BR-S05 — Give on behalf of someone (إهداء) *(Must)*
Any sponsorship or donation line can be dedicated to a named person. That person receives a
dedication card by SMS. This does **not** transfer the course or book to them. It is the
"تبرع عن غيرك" pattern used by Kafeef.

### BR-S06 — Zakat handled separately *(Must)*
Donors can mark a gift as zakat on eligible products. Zakat money is recorded, reported and spent
separately, and only on beneficiaries flagged as zakat-eligible.

### BR-S07 — One cart, one checkout, correct paperwork *(Must)*
Purchases and gifts share one cart and one payment. The system issues a **sales invoice** for
purchases and a **donation receipt** for gifts, never a single mixed document.

### BR-S08 — Show donors where their gift went *(Must)*
Every sponsored unit has a visible lifecycle: awaiting allocation → allocated → completed or
delivered. Donation products publish delivery updates, such as "cooler installed at school X", to
their donors.

### BR-S09 — Customer accounts with the lowest possible friction *(Must)*
Login is by mobile number and SMS one-time code. There are no passwords. The account holds
purchased courses, books, giving history and receipts.

### BR-S10 — Run the store without a developer *(Must)*
Staff manage products, prices, campaigns, stock, beneficiaries, allocations, shipments, refunds
and delivery updates from an admin area.

### BR-S11 — Finance-grade records *(Must)*
Every riyal is traceable from payment to order line to allocation. Reports are available for sales,
VAT, gifts by product, the zakat ledger and the allocation backlog. Audit logging covers every
administrative change.

### BR-S12 — Look and feel of ta3lom.sa *(Must)*
The store is part of ta3lom.sa, not a separate brand. It follows the approved prototypes, in
Arabic first and in English.

### BR-S13 — Be ready for the tax authority *(Should)*
If the association is VAT-registered, sales invoices must meet ZATCA e-invoicing (Fatoora) Phase 2.
The data model carries the required fields from day one. Integration is scheduled for S2.

### BR-S14 — Recurring and endowment giving *(Could, S2)*
Monthly sponsorship and waqf products, which FR-40 lists for the main site. Tokenised recurring
payments are deferred to S2.

## 4. Scope

### In scope for S1 (first release)

- Catalogue: courses, books (print and digital), donation products, sponsorship offers
- Product pages, search, category filters, share
- Server-side cart for guests and signed-in users
- Checkout with OTP sign-in, shipping address (print only), hosted payment page
- Payment webhook, reconciliation, refunds initiated by staff
- Course enrolment, digital downloads, print shipments
- Sponsorship pooling, beneficiary register, allocation console, seat status for donors
- Dedication cards (SMS and a public card page)
- Donation receipts, sales invoices (simplified tax invoice, PDF)
- Customer account: courses, books, giving, orders and receipts
- Admin: products, campaigns, stock, orders, refunds, beneficiaries, allocations, delivery updates, reports, audit log
- Integration with ta3lom.sa: header link, cart badge, `/donate` points to the store

### Out of scope for S1

| Item | Reason | When |
|---|---|---|
| Recurring or monthly donations, waqf | Needs tokenised cards and a mandate model | S2 |
| ZATCA Phase 2 API integration | Depends on VAT registration status (Q-02) | S2 |
| Coupons and discount codes | Not in the prototype | S2 |
| Course player / LMS | The store grants access; delivery is an adapter (Q-05) | Separate project |
| Multi-currency | SAR only | — |
| Marketplace (third-party sellers) | Not the association's model | — |
| Buy a course *for a named person* | Dropped at prototype review; dedication covers the need | Reconsider after launch |

## 5. Assumptions

1. Payments use a Saudi gateway that supports mada, Apple Pay, Visa/Mastercard and STC Pay through a **hosted payment page**. Card data never touches our servers.
2. The association can register an SMS sender ID with a Saudi SMS provider. This is needed for OTP and dedication cards.
3. All prices are in SAR and **include VAT** where VAT applies.
4. Gifts (sponsorships, donations) are outside the scope of VAT. **This must be confirmed by the association's accountant (Q-02).**
5. The store API runs on a Hostinger VPS at `store-api.ta3lom.sa`. The main site stays on Hostinger shared hosting.
6. The store's PostgreSQL database is separate from the main site's MySQL database. The two systems exchange data over HTTP only.

## 6. Constraints

| Constraint | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Hostinger **shared** hosting cannot run .NET | Store API on a VPS (ADR-S03) |
| `ta3lom-web` is **prerendered static** (ADR-002) | Catalogue pages are prerendered for SEO; prices, stock and goals load live; cart, checkout and account render client-side only |
| No visitor accounts exist on the main site (ADR-006) | Customer identity is new and lives only in the store (ADR-S04) |
| Saudi PDPL | Beneficiary personal data encrypted, minimised and never shown to donors |
| Saudi E-Commerce Law | Seller identity, VAT number, return policy and a 7-day withdrawal right for physical goods shown before purchase |
